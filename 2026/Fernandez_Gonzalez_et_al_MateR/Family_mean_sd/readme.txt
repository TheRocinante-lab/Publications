We have one script for each of the scenarios tested in the paper. Paper citation:

Fernández-González, J., Metwally, S. M., & y Sánchez, J. I. (2025). MateR: a novel framework for computing the usefulness criterion and applying genomic mating. bioRxiv, 2025-09.

Please note that this citation is just a pre-print, and at the date of writing this (15/12/2025) it is the only available version. The definitive paper will be published at Genetics.


TestMethods_clonal: clonal breeding program, diploid
TestMethods_clonal_Tetraploid: clonal breeding program, autotetraploid
TestMethods_DH: line breeding program, diploid, use double haploids
TestMethods_DH_Tester: hybrid breeding program, diploid, use double haploids
TestMethods_ILs: line breeding program, diploid, reach inbreeding by repeated selfing
TestMethods_ILs_Tester: hybrid breeding program, diploid, reach inbreeding by repeated selfing
TestMethods_ILs_Tester_tetraploid: hybrid breeding program, autotetraploid, reach inbreeding by repeated selfing
TestMethods_ILs_tetraploid: line breeding program, autotetraploid, reach inbreeding by repeated selfing
