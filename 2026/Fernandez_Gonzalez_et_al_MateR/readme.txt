This folder contains the code used for validating the equations in the MateR package. Citation:

Fernández-González, J., Metwally, S. M., & y Sánchez, J. I. (2025). MateR: a novel framework for computing the usefulness criterion and applying genomic mating. bioRxiv, 2025-09.

Please note that this citation is just a pre-print, and at the date of writing this (15/12/2025) it is the only available version. The definitive paper will be published at Genetics. Please, use it instead when available.
