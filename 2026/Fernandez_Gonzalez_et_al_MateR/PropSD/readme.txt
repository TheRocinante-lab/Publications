These scripts test the PropSD metric developed in the following paper:

Fernández-González, J., Metwally, S. M., & y Sánchez, J. I. (2025). MateR: a novel framework for computing the usefulness criterion and applying genomic mating. bioRxiv, 2025-09.

Please note that this citation is just a pre-print, and at the date of writing this (15/12/2025) it is the only available version. The definitive paper will be published at Genetics.


ExpectedSD.R: script with key helper functions for computing PropSD

sd_clonal.R: script that compares predicted to realized PropSD under selection in the framework of a clonal breeding program

sd_line.R: script that compares predicted to realized PropSD under selection in the framework of a line breeding program

sd_rand_mvrnorm.R: script that compares predicted to realized PropSD under random sampling.
